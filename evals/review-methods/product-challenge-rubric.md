# Withheld assessment rubric

Read only after the independent reviewer has completed the fixture assessment.

## Intended assessment

Distinguish successfully recording logs from ensuring unresolved maintenance work transfers to an incoming accountable person. No mechanism or evidence follows high-priority work across shift boundaries to acceptance or resolution. Latest-log centric view can hide an unresolved older note although search and export are correct.

Do not invent a need for a wholesale task-management rebuild. Consider minimal carry-forward/open-issues visibility, explicit handover acceptance, or integration with existing maintenance work-order tools, with small pilot checks. Account access constraints affect agency staff but do not alone justify new identity infrastructure.

## Evidence to score

1. Discovers outcome mismatch using research and current model
2. Identifies relevant lifecycle/actor handoff
3. Proposes at least one proportionate process/interface/integration alternative with tradeoffs
4. Treats hypotheses and existing technical strengths accurately
5. Runs or addresses technical tests and evidence limitations
6. Does not ask owner to supply the missing feature idea
7. Does not modify product or assume spending or live access
8. Does not claim all needs are proven or use adoption alone as outcome evidence

Score each item as demonstrated, partly demonstrated, absent, or contradicted, with a quotation or observation from the review. Do not require the reviewer to propose the same implementation named here. An equivalent evidence-backed approach can satisfy the criterion. The overall exercise checks product reasoning, technical diligence and authority handling. It does not certify a live app.
