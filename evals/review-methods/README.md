# Review method behavioural evaluations

These are synthetic, offline evaluations of skill behaviour. They do not certify
an app, a cloud run or universal compliance by every model.

Give a capable reviewer the selected skill, its referenced methods and one case.
Do not give expected findings before it writes its report. Use existing authority
and available capacity; running this evaluation does not authorise paid providers,
app changes or external messages. Retain the actual response and assess the
following independently:

- Does it investigate the requested outcome and adapt to the product profile?
- Does it discover material omissions or weaknesses from evidence, including ones
  not named by the user, and distinguish them from speculative opportunities?
- Does it propose credible approaches, including retaining or simplifying the
  current design, with tradeoffs and a way to test the underlying assumption?
- Does it continue the relevant technical review, including real contracts and
  affected consumers, rather than only writing a product critique?
- Does it respect read-only scope, preserve uncertainty and avoid treating a
  passed process, stale artifact or model verdict as proof of the intended effect?

Evaluate the reasoning and resulting behaviour, not keyword counts or resemblance
to a preferred feature list. No fixed suggestion quota or model score establishes
correctness. `expo-evidence-case.md` exercises evidence/recovery handling;
`library-case.md` exercises a non-UI product, compatible evolution, test blind
spots and restraint about unsupported new features. Product-discovery cases can
be authored independently and kept unseen by the method writer until evaluation.
