# Write the product brief

Recover the audience, problem, requested outcome, current decisions and constraints. Investigate factual gaps and ask about product choices only the user can settle.

Describe the real user journey, included and excluded scope, important failure/recovery behaviour and observable success. Distinguish decisions from proposals and unresolved questions. Include relevant accessibility, privacy, operating and business needs without turning every possibility into a requirement.

Keep the brief proportionate and usable by builders and checkers. Tie the proposed work to the original request. An approved brief does not automatically grant spending, publication or other authority beyond the user's instruction.

The user's current request and host permissions govern. This skill adds expertise, not authority or a mandatory separate workflow.
