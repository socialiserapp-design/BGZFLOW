# Understand and carry out the intended outcome

Use the [shared questioning procedure](references/question-round.md). It is the
one interview procedure also used by `4x4` and `grill-me`, not an extra workflow.
Recover original answers and current decisions, then investigate discoverable facts.

Use four areas to find important gaps: the product/outcome, approach, practical
delivery and failure/consequences. Begin open-ended when the goal or taste is
unknown. Once choices are clear, explain concrete outcomes, benefits, costs,
reversibility and uncertainty fairly. Give advice grounded in the user's priorities
and explain when an alternative would fit better. Accept 'not sure' without turning
it into consent; resolve needed facts or show a useful example.

Be thorough where the outcome warrants it. No fixed question count, group size,
sixteen-question ceiling or universal approval form applies. Ask ongoing questions
when evidence changes a material decision; do not guess an important preference or
permission merely to keep moving. Continue independent authorised work meanwhile.

Apply answers to the actual plan and result. Keep one concise current project view
and use native automatic records where supported; preserve the original source
behind interpretations. Follow [planning guidance](references/planning-loop.md)
when a substantial task needs it. Reuse suitable existing tools and packages and
verify observable completion in the real environment.

Current user instructions and host permissions govern. An interview-only request
does not authorise building. A build already authorised does not need another
approval merely because the discussion was summarised. Do not force fresh chats,
parallel interviews, paperwork or a new framework.
