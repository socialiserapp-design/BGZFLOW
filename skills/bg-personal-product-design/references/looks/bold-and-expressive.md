# Looks: bold and expressive

Part of the [looks library](../looks-library.md). Every value is a starting point for a direction, never a default. A product's approved look and tokens always win.

### B1 Neubrutalism
**Suits:** youth brands, creative tools, collaboration apps, indie launches. **Avoid for:** luxury, healthcare, conservative finance.
- **Type:** bold grotesk or rounded sans (Space Grotesk, Archivo, Lexend), heavy weights.
- **Colour:** saturated flats (yellow `#FFEB3B`, red `#FF5252`, blue `#2196F3`) with black outlines; no gradients.
- **Space and shape:** 2-3 px black borders, hard offset shadow `4px 4px 0 #000`, radius 0 or small.
- **Material:** flat colour only.
- **Motion:** press moves the element into its shadow (`translate(4px,4px)`, shadow to 0); instant hovers.
- **Signature:** sticker-like badges, chunky tabs, highlighted words with a block behind them.

### B2 Bauhaus Geometric
**Suits:** mobile apps that need strong personality, onboarding, education, cultural brands. **Avoid for:** subtle premium.
- **Type:** geometric sans (Outfit, Jost) with very heavy hero weight.
- **Colour:** primary red `#D02020`, blue `#1040C0`, yellow `#F0C020`, off-white `#F0F0F0`, stark black `#121212`.
- **Space and shape:** circles, squares and triangles as layout blocks; radius 0 for blocks and full pill for buttons; 2 px borders.
- **Material:** hard offset shadow `4px 4px 0 #121212`, dot-grid section pattern.
- **Motion:** mechanical press (translate), instant hover, slide-over transitions.
- **Signature:** composition built from primary shapes that also carry content.

### B3 Memphis
**Suits:** music, events, youth campaigns, creative agencies. **Avoid for:** legal, finance, older audiences.
- **Type:** chunky display with playful rotation.
- **Colour:** hot pink `#FF71CE`, yellow `#FFCE5C`, teal `#86CCCA`, blue-purple `#6A7BB4`.
- **Space and shape:** scattered geometric confetti (squiggles, triangles, dots) rotated about 15 degrees, clip-path shapes.
- **Material:** repeating patterns, blend modes.
- **Motion:** floating shapes on scroll (paused off-screen and under reduced motion).
- **Signature:** squiggle and dot patterns framing photos.

### B4 Vibrant Block
**Suits:** startups, social, gaming, entertainment, youth products. **Avoid for:** trust-first services.
- **Type:** big bold sans, colour-on-colour headlines.
- **Colour:** full-bleed saturated colour fields per section with high-contrast text.
- **Space and shape:** big blocks, rounded 16-24 or sharp by brand.
- **Material:** flat.
- **Motion:** section colour transitions on scroll, bouncy entries.
- **Signature:** each section owns a colour; the page reads like a deck of coloured cards.

### B5 Maximalist Chaos
**Suits:** music artists, fashion drops, viral campaigns. **Avoid for:** anything with tasks or money.
- **Type:** mixed families, stretched and distorted display.
- **Colour:** clashing brights (`#FF00FF`, `#00FF00`, `#FFFF00`, `#0000FF`), rainbow and noise.
- **Space and shape:** sticker layering, overlap, collage; drag-and-drop elements.
- **Material:** GIF-like loops, glitch, noise.
- **Motion:** marquees, jitter (about 5 degrees), all stopped under reduced motion.
- **Signature:** a page that feels like a scrapbook you can rearrange.

### B6 Y2K Chrome
**Suits:** fashion, music, nostalgia campaigns, youth brands. **Avoid for:** B2B, health.
- **Type:** wide extended or bubbly display, small pixel-like UI type.
- **Colour:** hot pink `#FF69B4`, cyan `#00FFFF`, silver `#C0C0C0`, purple `#9400D3`; iridescent gradients.
- **Space and shape:** bubble shapes, pill buttons, star bursts.
- **Material:** chrome and glossy highlights (`linear-gradient(180deg, white 0%, transparent 50%)`), 3D chrome type.
- **Motion:** shimmer and glow pulses.
- **Signature:** chrome logo type and glossy jelly buttons.

### B7 Retro-Futurism and Synthwave
**Suits:** gaming, music, tech events, entertainment. **Avoid for:** conservative products, long reading.
- **Type:** retro display or mono with neon text shadow.
- **Colour:** deep night `#1A1A2E`, neon blue `#0080FF`, hot pink `#FF006E`, cyan; sunset gradients.
- **Space and shape:** perspective grid floors, sun discs, chrome frames.
- **Material:** neon glow (text-shadow plus box-shadow), CRT scanlines.
- **Motion:** glitch keyframes (skew and offset), grid scroll; stop under reduced motion.
- **Signature:** a horizon grid that moves with scroll.

### B8 Pixel Art
**Suits:** indie games, retro tools, playful communities. **Avoid for:** corporate, high-res photography.
- **Type:** a pixel font at integer sizes for display, a readable sans for long text.
- **Colour:** limited palette (NES-like), black outlines, dithering for shade.
- **Space and shape:** 4 px pixel unit, stepped corners, `image-rendering: pixelated`.
- **Material:** none beyond pixels; no anti-aliasing.
- **Motion:** frame-by-frame sprites, instant cuts, blinking cursor.
- **Signature:** an animated sprite mascot reacting to actions.

### B9 Vintage Film
**Suits:** photography, vinyl and music brands, travel journals, nostalgic food. **Avoid for:** SaaS, children.
- **Type:** classic serif or typewriter with a clean sans for UI.
- **Colour:** faded cream `#F5E6C8`, sepia `#D4A574`, muted teal `#4A7B7C`, dusty pink `#E8B4B8`.
- **Space and shape:** polaroid and film-frame borders, small radii.
- **Material:** film grain about 15%, light leaks, slight desaturation (saturation about 0.8, contrast about 1.1).
- **Motion:** gentle fade-ins, polaroid shake, optional light-leak sweep.
- **Signature:** photos framed as prints with handwritten captions.

### B10 Kinetic Brutalism (mobile)
**Suits:** culture and music apps, brand flagship apps, immersive stories. **Avoid for:** utilities.
- **Type:** huge condensed uppercase that fills the screen width.
- **Colour:** black and white with one acid accent.
- **Space and shape:** edge-to-edge type blocks, radius 0.
- **Material:** none.
- **Motion:** type that slides, scales and stacks with scroll and gestures; haptic tick on snap.
- **Signature:** navigation by giant words.
