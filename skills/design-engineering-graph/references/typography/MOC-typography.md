# MOC — Typography

Typography is where AI-generated UIs give themselves away first. Inter and SF Pro are tells.

- [line-length-tracking](line-length-tracking.md) — 45-75 chars per line. Tighten tracking as size increases. text-wrap: pretty. Uppercase rules.
