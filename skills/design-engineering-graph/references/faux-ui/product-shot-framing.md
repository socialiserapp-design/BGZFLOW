# Product-shot framing

Framing decides whether the picture reads as a product shot or a screenshot pasted into a box. Resolve consequential framing uncertainty before dependent implementation; the actual crop affects what needs building and how large type must appear.

## Size first

Inspect at the size the audience will actually see. The source examples are 720×340 beside copy, 900–1100px wide for a full hero and 400×300 for a feature card; smaller demos can also work if their meaning remains readable. Crop, recompose or simplify when scaling would hide the important detail. These dimensions do not add breakpoints or deliverables to the brief.

## The rules

- Establish a useful focal hierarchy. A hero and one or two satellites is a source recipe, not a count limit; balanced comparisons can use another arrangement.
- Preserve what explains the actual product, action and outcome. A decorative editorial crop may hide filler; do not crop required information or functional controls out of a claimed usable experience.
- Choose intentional bleed or intentional margins. The source's 10–40% crop and two-thirds stage width are examples. A whole window, asymmetry, empty corner or frontal/tilted composition may fit the approved direction.
- Preserve proportions unless deliberate distortion is part of the illustration. Read actual overlays and popovers in context, with space to avoid unintended clipping.
- If the illustration uses an overflow mask, check all affected states. A mask is not authority to remove requested content, controls or interaction.
- Use coherent gutters, z-order, shadows, backgrounds and radii for the chosen style; no mandatory single-gutter, satellite leader or starting-flat rule applies.

## The page is the ground

A transparent stage can blend a product shot into the page; a plate, framed window or contrasting ground can be equally deliberate. Check contrast, edge clipping and actual hierarchy. Nested grounds and even margins are not defects by themselves. Do not change the page around an isolated illustration unless that work is part of the request.

## Layouts

| Layout | Hero | Satellite | Use for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Corner bleed | Inset ~10% top and left, 15–35% past right and bottom | Straddling left or bottom | Hero beside copy |
| Pinned, bottom bleed | Inset 8–12% left, right, top; 15–35% past the bottom | Optional, straddling a side | Feature card, bento tile |
| Stage with gutters | Inset 8–20% each side on a coloured stage, off the bottom | One side | Brand moment |
| Satellite focus | Cropped hard, mostly context | Large, 30–40% of hero width, carries the story | A notification, receipt, diff |
| Wide hero | 900–1100px at 1:1, realistic fidelity, off the bottom | A card on its lower edge | Full-width homepage hero |
| Device pair | Desktop ~75–80% wide, off the bottom | Phone straddling right, a third outside, present from frame one | Cross-device story |
| Close-up | Whole window at 1.3–1.6×, chrome and all | None | A card about one control |
| Many of one | A grid of one small thing off every edge, one in focus by depth | None | Templates, previews, sessions |
| Tilted stack | Three cards at −6°, 4°, −2°, offset 12–20px; only the top one has words | None | Paper: receipts, tickets — the one place tilt earns its keep |
| Corner chip | Window inset top-left | A pill centred on its corner, half outside | One live fact about the whole product |

These are source layout recipes, not mandatory counts, dimensions, crop ratios or the only valid reasons to tilt. A whole window with even gutters can be valid when its contents read and it serves the approved composition.

## Phones

Frame the actual platform and input at readable shown size. The source's 40%-width/220px phone, iOS notification margins and cropped 400px-host composition are examples, not universal device requirements. A realistic platform study should preserve correct system geometry and safe areas; a clearly stylized illustration can simplify. Do not force iOS chrome on Android or resize actual UI into a misleading claim.

## Compact is a second design

Check the actual responsive locations where this demo ships. If downscaling makes text or the payoff unreadable, recompose, crop closer or stack supporting objects. A separate compact composition can help, but is not automatically required for every demo. The source's 358px column in a 390px viewport is an example; use the real host widths. See [viewport-custom-design](../layout/viewport-custom-design.md).

## When to apply

Use when framing affects a requested hero illustration, feature card, docs plate or bento tile. Recheck relevant composition changes; no required pre-code planning ceremony.

## Gotcha

A barely clipped edge may look accidental, while aggressive bleed can hide the point. Judge the actual shown composition rather than obeying a fixed crop fraction or copying another product shot.
