# Launch-video formats

Create only the film variants requested or already authorized. Feature loops, logo stings, square and vertical edits serve different viewing conditions; none is an automatic addition to a completed film.

## Feature loop

For a requested loop, make the opening/poster and reset intelligible, and inspect at the actual embedded size. An endless cycle, explicit restart or play-once behavior can each fit. Do not require no-fade seams or a name/action within two seconds regardless of purpose. Measure a loop seam against comparable frames when useful, distinguishing intended change from a glitch.

For GIF export, centisecond timing means 25fps (4cs) and 50fps (2cs) are exact; other rates may require alternating frame delays. Choose frame rate, dimensions and size for the actual destination, and verify the saved animation rather than imposing a universal megabyte or width cap.

## Logo sting

A short reveal can use mark construction, type, a material transformation or simple stillness. The source's 3–8s durations, 28–45% frame occupancy and 1.5s holds are examples, not caps or required counts. Preserve the approved identity and legibility. Supply transparency, sound or additional encodes only when the actual use calls for them; inspect alpha edges and playback if supplied.

## Vertical and square

Adapt the requested aspect ratio to the actual destination. Re-layout, crop or letterbox can each be appropriate if they preserve the important action and readable composition. Confirm current platform overlays/safe areas rather than treating inherited feed margins as permanent facts. There is no requirement to fill the bottom 40% or add motion to empty space.

Provide captions when needed for the viewing situation and accessibility, using supported caption tracks or burned-in text as appropriate. Check actual mobile shown size, occlusion and timing. A centered landscape encode does not by itself prove a successful vertical adaptation, but intentional letterboxing is not automatically a defect.

## Gotcha

The zones are snapshots of the big feeds' current overlays (header 240–290 px, action rail 120–190 px from about y 840, caption block 380–450 px, up to ~670 with a call-to-action button). They move when the apps redesign. Check the destination's overlay before a paid launch.
