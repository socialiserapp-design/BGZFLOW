## Its register is not this graph's kinetic-type row

Its references are high-energy motion-design reels: the fastest is 16 % still, has a longest still of 0.37 s, and cuts 9.1 times per 10 s (55 a minute). Kaiju Postal, its own teaser, cuts 75 a minute. That sits at or past the fast edge of this graph's **beat-cut sizzle** row (30.7 a minute, up to 60.6), not in its **kinetic type** row, whose three films median 3.3 cuts a minute. So `finish.py`'s gates (≤ 40 % still, no still over 0.6 s before the end card, median motion ≥ 2.5 px a frame) are that source's house checks, not universal requirements even within an energetic register. A product-camera film is 49 % still and would fail them.

Its still share is not this graph's still share either: it counts a frame as still when under 1.5 % of a 320 × 180 frame changes, while [launch-video-review](launch-video-review.md) counts one only when nothing visible changes. Compare its numbers with its own references only. ## What it adds

- **Outline, then fill:** an outlined word for something not yet true fills on its beat (NO TIMELINE.).
- **Runs of beat cuts accelerate** (1, 1, 1, ½, ½, ¼ beat, then a flash), and a run can flip the ground on every cut.
- **A custom sound for the film's own joke** (a knock, a footstep, a bell) earns more than another stock impact ([designed-sfx-anatomy](../sound/designed-sfx-anatomy.md)).
- **Key art:** choose licensed existing or authorized generated media. Compose empty space, overlays or darkening when useful to the brief; inspect actual images, rights and misleading marks before use.
