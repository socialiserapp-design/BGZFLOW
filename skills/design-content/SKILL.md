# Make the interface say what it means

Recover the intended action, real state, audience and established terminology.
For a clear wording change, make it directly within scope. Do not start a visual
redesign or another interview because this specialist was selected.

Use a label that predicts the actual action and distinguish navigation from an
operation. Put necessary explanations where the decision happens. Before offering
an option, explain its meaningful result, cost or consequence and whether it can
be reversed; do not make one choice sound safer or more capable without evidence.
Preserve concise conventional wording when it is clearer than a clever alternative.

For forms, distinguish labels, examples, requirements and help. Accept harmless
format variation that the actual system supports. On error, identify what needs
attention, explain how to recover and preserve valid input. Choose validation
timing for the interaction rather than importing one site's rules. Associate
messages with the relevant field and preserve reading/focus order.

Tie success and progress language to observed state. Queued, sent, accepted and
saved can mean different things. A missing reply is an unknown outcome; explain
the next reconciliation step without claiming failure or inviting duplicate work.
For AI features, describe actual activity and uncertainty, not fabricated thoughts,
confidence percentages or promises of accuracy. Use provider names only where
the product and user's decisions call for them.

Test the words in the rendered component with realistic data, supported languages,
text expansion and right-to-left layout where applicable. Use locale-aware dates,
numbers, plural forms and meaningful units; avoid sentence fragments assembled
in ways translation cannot reorder. Keep visible and accessible names aligned.
Do not assume that a shorter English phrase solves a layout problem in every locale.

Check the relevant success, error and recovery actions after changing their copy.
Retain important terminology or user decisions in the existing design source.
A polished message cannot repair a broken operation: use
[product flows](../design-product-flows/SKILL.md) or engineering diagnosis for that.
Use the [shared contract](../bg-personal-product-design/references/design-contract.md)
without introducing another record or approval ritual. [Sources](../../docs/design-skill-sources.md).
