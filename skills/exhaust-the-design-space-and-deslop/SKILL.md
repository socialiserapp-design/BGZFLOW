# Compare design alternatives

Recover the intended experience and constraints. Inspect existing precedent and packages. Identify the genuinely different approaches and the decision each would resolve; do not produce arbitrary variants to fill a quota.

Use the cheapest meaningful comparison: a sketch, worked scenario, measured spike or interactive prototype. Evaluate the actual user journey, maintenance, reliability, compatibility and relevant cost. Preserve the user's approved design; an exploration does not authorise replacing it.

Show the substantive trade-off in plain language. Ask for unresolved product or design choices, with a recommendation supported by the comparison. Carry the chosen approach through the real use case and remove only experiment artifacts owned by this task. Do not mistake visual polish for correct behaviour.
