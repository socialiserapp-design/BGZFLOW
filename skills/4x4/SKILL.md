# Four areas, adaptive depth

This is a BGZFLOW adaptation of David Ondrej's 4x4 method. The four areas organise
coverage; they are not four compulsory rounds of four questions. Ask more than
sixteen when important questions remain, and fewer when the next step is clear.

Follow the [shared questioning procedure](../bg-plain-english-builder/references/question-round.md)
used by `bg-plain-english-builder` and `grill-me`. Do not run a second interview
when one of those skills is already active. Recover current decisions and answers,
then explore the outcome, approach, practical delivery and failure consequences.

Start open-ended when the person's goal or taste is unknown. For a material choice,
explain each credible option's actual outcome, upside, cost or risk, reversibility
and uncertain facts on the same criteria. Ground the recommendation in the user's
priorities and state when an alternative would win. Do not manufacture options,
bundle consent or interpret uncertainty as permission.

Investigate discoverable facts and use a proportionate example or prototype where
it resolves uncertainty. Apply answers to the actual work; revisit affected choices
when new evidence or corrections arrive. Continue independent authorised work,
but keep consequential dependent choices unresolved until they are settled.

The current request determines whether this is discussion, planning or part of an
already authorised build. This skill creates no blanket stop after the interview,
extra approval, manual record requirement or forced new chat.
