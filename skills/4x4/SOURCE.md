# Source and adaptation

Based on David Ondrej's [4x4 skill](https://github.com/davidondrej/skills/blob/ec0b0c87d016d0db0260b0c7368624897baaea39/skills/thinking-and-docs/4x4/SKILL.md).
The source and its MIT licence were inspected. The complete upstream notice is
retained in [UPSTREAM-LICENSE](UPSTREAM-LICENSE).

BGZFLOW retains four coverage areas and adapts the approach to one shared ongoing
questioning procedure. It does not retain a fixed sixteen-question count, a ban
on options/recommendations, a ban on research, a prohibition on success questions,
guessing vague answers, or the limit on follow-up questions. It also does not
create a new approval boundary after an already authorised build discussion.

Matt Pocock's dependency-aware grilling and Superpowers' open-ended discovery
inform the shared procedure; their fixed interview/approval workflows are not
imported. Research informs fair presentation and useful clarification, not a
claim that a research model was implemented. Details and limitations are in
[the method note](../../docs/questioning-method.md).
