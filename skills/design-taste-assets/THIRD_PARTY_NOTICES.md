## Photographs

`asset-rights.json` maps every bundled photograph to its author, supplied title or
description, source page, licence and modification note. Original photo and layout
credit indexes are also retained. Some image files are upstream resized/re-encoded
renditions, as recorded. A Public Domain Mark is a public-domain status marker,
not the same legal tool as CC0. The source's rights metadata is retained evidence;
it is not a new independent verification of every photograph's ownership or releases.

Bundled photographs carry these different terms: CC0; Public Domain Mark1.0;
CC BY2.0,3.0,4.0; CC BY-SA2.0,3.0,4.0 and3.0 AT; and the Unsplash License.
Keep the applicable credit, licence link and modification information. Adapted
share-alike photos retain their applicable share-alike licence; they are not
relicensed by this toolkit. CC legal texts are retained in `LICENSES/`, with
the canonical licence URL in each asset's record.

Unsplash terms: https://unsplash.com/license . Those photographs are not covered
by MIT. Unsplash permits broad use but restricts selling unmodified images and
compiling them to reproduce a similar/competing image service. Library frames
must not be misrepresented as the client's own premises, staff or product.
Project-specific privacy, property, model or mark permissions are separate from
an image's stated copyright licence. No remote-only photo was fetched by this import.

## Icons and trademarks

The bundled icon index identifies pinned source sets:

- Gil Barbara logos at the recorded commit: CC0-1.0.
- Simple Icons16.30.0: CC0-1.0.
- vscode-icons through Iconify1.2.30: MIT.
- Lucide through Iconify1.2.100: ISC, with the additional Feather MIT notice for
  the icons identified in its licence file.

Full retained notices are in `LICENSES/`. `asset-rights.json` maps local SVGs to
the declared set; five older demo glyph names absent from the curated index are
explicitly listed there, retaining the upstream Lucide-set provenance. Original
brand drawings can remain trademarks even when their drawing copyright is CC0.
Use real product marks truthfully; do not imply endorsement or a supported
integration that does not exist. Do not recolour a mark solely to obey a template.

## Fonts and export

Font pairing records and Google Fonts links are examples. Font binaries are not
bundled; obtain the actual selected font licence if adding files to a product.
There is no automatic font or photo download. The export helper preserves this
notice, all retained licence texts, all original credit indexes and the per-file
rights map. Do not discard those files merely because only one asset is used.
