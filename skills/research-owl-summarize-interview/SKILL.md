# Summarize Interview

Use this method only for the current research question. Reuse the existing brief, applicable user answers and private records; several selected methods contribute to one investigation, not repeated interviews. Inspect permitted actual evidence and preserve source links. Do not invent participants, quotations, emotions, findings, measurements or approvals; mark hypotheses and missing data explicitly. Planning a study does not authorise recruitment, messages, recording, tracking installation, spending, account changes or product changes. The current task and host permissions govern; shared automatic records are not proof that a study ran.

Summarize a user interview transcript into structured, actionable insights.

## Instructions

1. **Read the transcript** carefully, noting key moments.
2. **Create a structured summary** with:
   - **Participant profile**: Role, context, experience level
   - **Key themes**: actual themes supported by this transcript, with supporting quotes
   - **Jobs-to-be-done**: What the participant is trying to accomplish
   - **Pain points**: Frustrations, barriers, and unmet needs (with severity)
   - **Workarounds**: How they currently solve problems
   - **Delighters**: What works well or exceeds expectations
   - **Notable quotes**: useful verbatim quotations actually present in the permitted source, with traceable locations
   - **Surprises**: Anything unexpected or counter to assumptions
   - **Action items**: Specific design or research follow-ups suggested by the findings
3. **Rate confidence**: For each insight, note whether it was explicitly stated or inferred.
4. Present in a clear, scannable format for the authorised audience in the existing record. Preparing a summary does not authorise sharing participant material.
