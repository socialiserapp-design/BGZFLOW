# Authorship and contributions

## Project creator and lead

**[Simeon Kontogeorges](https://github.com/socialiserapp-design)** created and leads
BGZFLOW. His work establishes the product vision, workflow requirements,
integration direction and real-project acceptance goals.

Public profiles: [GitHub](https://github.com/socialiserapp-design),
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simeon-konto-soclo/),
[X](https://x.com/socloapp),
[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/socloapp/).

BGZFLOW's original system work connects:

- intent-led selection from a large, selectively loaded method library;
- one shared contract for scope, decisions, authority and evidence;
- host-aware context continuity and source-backed follow-up recovery;
- narrow mechanical safety checks alongside host permissions;
- tests of actual outcomes and interrupted work;
- method adaptations, conflict handling, provenance and portable packaging.

Implementation, evaluation and substantial adaptations were developed with AI
assistance under Simeon's direction.
