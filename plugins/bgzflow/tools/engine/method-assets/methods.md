# Shared methods

## Understand and plan

Recover the intended outcome, audience, environment, current decisions and meaningful success checks. Distinguish user instructions, agent proposals and ordinary implementation choices. Ask the smallest question that resolves a consequential ambiguity; investigate facts independently. Verify that answers actually affect the plan.

Inspect existing work and compare relevant alternatives when the choice matters. Prefer suitable established tools and packages. Explain significant trade-offs. Keep one concise plan of complete usable outcomes and choose an appropriate single-agent or multi-agent approach from actual available resources.

## Research and check

Identify the claim or uncertainty that could change the work. Inspect applicable primary sources, original records and real system state. Check supporting passages, relevant dates/versions and material contradictions. Distinguish observation, source-backed conclusions and inference.

Define the intended observable effect, then inspect it in the real environment. Check relevant failure and recovery behaviour. Use independent scrutiny where appropriate. Preserve failed checks and say what could not be verified; another model's agreement or a generated record is not proof.

## Continue and recover

Recover original intent, latest corrections, task ownership, retained work and pending operations after native compaction or interruption. Inspect the actual current state before relying on a historical marker. Restore relevant methods when needed; do not force a fresh chat or replay unrelated history.

Reconcile uncertain external actions under their original identity before retrying. Reuse valid completed work. Diagnose repeated failures and change the supported approach rather than blindly repeating it. Resume the next authorised unfinished action and verify the result against the original request.
