# Find a defensible quality benchmark

Apply this during substantial planning, when evidence changes the approach, and
in the existing completion review, without waiting for “what is missing?”. Reuse
recent relevant evidence for small changes. Seek the strongest supported outcome
for the user's purpose and constraints, not imitation or endless polishing.

## Discover the comparison

Reconstruct the job, audience and environment. Find missing expertise, neglected
actors/journeys, false assumptions and better concepts through the full-spectrum
product challenge. Look beyond the supplied feature list. Investigate consequential
uncertainties using current primary guidance, relevant standards, strong comparable
products and maintained implementations. Distinguish observed behaviour from a
vendor claim or inferred architecture. Verify date, version, platform, scale and
account limitations. Model memory or a search snippet cannot establish superiority.
Use accessible sources if a reference needs new spending/access; state the gap.

Use the smallest reference set that tests competing explanations, including a
simpler approach and a materially different approach when useful. Seek evidence
against the favourite. Popularity is not proof of suitability; borrow principles,
not proprietary assets. Select references for the actual domain, for example:

- **UI/UX:** approved taste and direction, comparable complete flows from Mobbin
  or accessible product examples, platform design guidance, usability research
  and accessibility standards. Compare clarity, hierarchy, feedback, states and
  interactions on relevant devices. Screenshots cannot prove usability or data
  effects; the owner still chooses taste.
- **Architecture:** current provider contracts, maintained implementations and
  operational guidance. Compare workload, compatibility, failure/recovery, data,
  security, maintenance, latency and total cost. Fashionable patterns do not prove
  better outcomes.
- **Content/AI:** trusted source material, professional expertise, audience needs
  and representative accepted/rejected examples. Compare actual outputs and hard
  cases, including first-ranked results; inspect all relevant modalities.
- **Other work:** discover that domain's standards and suitable evaluator rather
  than applying software metrics indiscriminately.

## Turn research into checks

In the existing brief/rubric, record the reference, what was observed, the candidate's
gap, its user consequence and how to verify it. Separate required correctness and
promises, evidence-supported quality targets, optional improvements and decisions
that need the owner. Do not invent a universal 8/10 or numeric target from reputation.
For material qualitative choices, use anchored criteria and blind comparisons
where practical. Give independent evaluators the original objective, references
and actual result. Jev or another suitable judge can compare evidence; it cannot
supply missing observations or make preference objective.

Measure candidate and comparator under comparable conditions where possible:
cold/warm use, relevant device/network/data, sample size and variation. For a
multi-provider model picker, investigate discovery/refresh, account entitlement,
capabilities, usage/reset visibility, cache identity, staleness, provider failure
and interaction speed. Public model metadata is not an account's live allowance.
Test new/removed models, account changes, warm/cold opens and offline recovery.
Prefer supported APIs/events to guessed limits or private-endpoint scraping.
Consider immediate cached choices with independent refresh when appropriate;
verify the actual behaviour rather than attributing this design to a closed app.

## Close the loop

Before accepting substantial work, identify the strongest material gap found,
why to repair or retain the current approach, and remaining uncertainty. “No
material gap found in the examined scope” is valid with evidence. No defect quota.
Return confirmed failures of authorised outcomes to the existing builder; present
scope/design changes with trade-offs. Do not silently lower the standard, raise it
after every pass, or require every competitor feature. Apply existing release rules.

Refresh relevant research when external capabilities change, evidence goes stale,
a correction reveals misunderstanding or an escaped defect exposes a missing
criterion. Test the improved evaluator on that failure and unfamiliar cases.
Report scoped quality, not a guarantee of the universally best possible product.
