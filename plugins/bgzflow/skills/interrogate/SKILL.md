# Challenge the work

Recover the actual request, consequential answers, constraints and candidate. Give a checker neutral source material: the actual change, relevant callers, intended behaviour and evidence. Do not prime it to endorse the builder or find a quota of bugs.

Choose independent checking proportionate to consequence. Independent areas may use separate reviewers when capacity allows; one careful checker can be enough. A different provider may reveal a different blind spot, but is not proof of independence or truth.

For each finding, verify the trigger is reachable in the actual system, identify the observable consequence and provide a reproduction or direct evidence. Investigate conflicting findings rather than counting votes. Reject claims contradicted by callers, current APIs or the actual brief. Do not discard a rare but consequential failure merely because a numerical score is small.

Distinguish demonstrated defects, unresolved risks and optional improvements. Prioritise wrong money, lost data, broken permissions, privacy, user-visible failures and unsupported scope. Report the strongest findings clearly; zero findings is valid. Review-only work does not authorise repairs or release.

Rate material findings with separate **harm 1–10** and **how often 1–10**, using
[the shared impact ratings](references/impact-ratings.md). Keep evidence status,
affected scope, occurrence denominator, recovery and urgency distinct; use unknown
when unsupported. Apply the same method to misleading claims, documents, marketing
and privacy concerns. Ratings support this review, not another review or an
automatic permission, priority formula or customer-facing hold.
